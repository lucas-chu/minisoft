**I’d build MiniHackathon first, #33 below:** you already know the workflow and have people who could test it.

The broader rule: **replace the expensive workflow someone actually uses, not every feature of the enterprise suite.** Use AI to build and configure the software, without requiring an AI call for every click.

## How I’d structure Minisoft

**One shared foundation, not 100 unrelated codebases.** Reuse permissions, imports, file storage, backups, audit logs, and tests; make the products configurations and specialized modules.

The biggest regret risk is turning cheap generation into **100 maintenance obligations**. Keep ordinary workflows usable without live model calls, and reserve your GPU experiments for generation, evaluation, transcription, and other genuinely compute-heavy work. **“Almost-free to generate” should be the starting hypothesis, not a promise that operating the software costs nothing.**
